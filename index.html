Nutrition · Modules 1 to 14

Nutrition Study Tools

See nutrition happen inside the body. Each module has an animated walkthrough that follows food from the plate to the cell, with worked examples for every calculation, and a Lab with simulators, a practice trainer, a sorting game and real-life care scenarios. Everything runs in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. No login, nothing graded.

How to use this: open the walkthrough first and go at your own pace with the arrow keys or Next. Then open the Lab: run the simulator, practice in the trainer with its hints and worked solutions, and try the real-life scenarios. Use the tools bar at the top of each page to pause the animations, slow them down, have the text read aloud, or get a written description of each picture. For learning only, not personal medical or diet advice.
