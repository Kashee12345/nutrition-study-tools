Part 1: Bacteria Growth Simulator

Simulator readouts
ReadingValueWhat it means

A teaching model, not a food safety test. Bacteria are drawn as one colony that doubles; the count starts at 100. Doubling time is about 20 minutes between 70 and 110 °F and slower toward 40 and 140 °F; real microbes vary and often have a slow start (lag phase), which this model leaves out to show the worst case. Cooking to the safe temperature is shown as killing nearly all bacteria, but some spores survive and some toxins are not destroyed by heat.
