Part 1: Vitamin D and Calcium Simulator

Gold vitamin D discs come from the skin (sunlight) and from food or supplements. The liver turns them amber (25-hydroxyvitamin D), and the kidney makes red calcitriol when PTH (blue) calls for it. Calcitriol helps the gut absorb calcium (grey) for blood and bone.
