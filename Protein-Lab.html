MeasureValue

A teaching model, not a personal prescription. It treats about 0.66 g/kg per day as the protein a healthy adult loses and must replace, raises losses in illness and when energy intake is low, and lets growth or strength training add new body protein when energy is adequate. Extra protein beyond that is deaminated, so it raises nitrogen out, not body protein. One simulated day passes each second. Energy needs are a rough population estimate (about 30 kcal per kg) used only to show protein as a percent of calories.
